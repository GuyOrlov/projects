Accessibility statement

Access to Money is designed to make money information easier to read, navigate and understand.

Our accessibility approach

We are designing Access to Money to work with keyboard navigation, screen magnification, screen readers, high-contrast settings, responsive layouts and plain-language content. We also plan BSL-supported versions of key guidance.

Our target: WCAG 2.2 AA. We continue to test and improve the site and do not claim that every part is perfect yet.

Accessibility controls

Use the A+ button in the header to increase text size. Use the ◐ button to switch between light and dark modes. Your choice is saved on your device.

Tell us about a barrier

Email hello@accesstomoney.co.uk. Please tell us which page you were using, what you were trying to do and what went wrong.
