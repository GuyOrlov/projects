Why can the percentages add up to more than 100%?

A person can report more than one impairment. For example, someone may report both mobility and stamina difficulties. These percentages describe reported impairment types, not separate groups of people.

How to read this page

The labels are broad, and people can have more than one impairment.

Different surveys and public services may use different categories. This page keeps the source definition and age group visible so the figures are not treated as if they measure exactly the same thing.
