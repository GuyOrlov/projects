About this project

UK Disability Data Hub is an Access to Data project created by Guy Orlov. It brings together public statistics to make disability-related data easier to read and explore.

Information, not advice

The site is for general information and data exploration. It is not legal, financial, medical, benefits or other professional advice.

Data and sources

Figures are based on the public sources linked alongside the data, including government and official statistical publications. Different sources may use different definitions, populations, geographic coverage and time periods. Check the linked original source before relying on a figure for an important decision.

Accuracy and updates

Reasonable care is taken to present figures clearly and accurately, but data may be revised, updated or corrected by its original publisher. The site may also be updated without notice.

External links

Links to third-party and government websites are provided for reference. Access to Data does not control those websites or their availability.
