Neutral evidence approach

Show what the evidence says — and what it does not say.

Access to Data separates facts from interpretation. A government statistic, a census count, an administrative record and a research finding are labelled differently. Where evidence is incomplete or definitions differ, that limitation stays visible.

Read the full evidence approach →
Official statistic / surveyPopulation estimates with source, period and methodology.
CensusPopulation counts using the census question and definition.
Administrative dataPeople recorded by a service, benefit or public system.
Research findingClearly attributed evidence from research or an organisation; never presented as an official statistic.
