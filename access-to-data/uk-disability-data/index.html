Methodology first

Different sources do not always measure the same thing.

Survey estimates, census counts and administrative caseloads are kept separate. Geography, time period and population coverage stay attached to each number so the dashboard does not imply false comparability.

SurveyPopulation estimates, often with sampling uncertainty.
CensusPopulation counts based on census questions and definitions.
AdministrativePeople recorded by a service or benefit system, such as PIP.
