How we use the data

We separate the facts from the explanation.

Different sources measure different things. We label surveys, census figures, public-service records and research clearly. We also point out when figures cannot be compared directly or when the data has limits.

Read how we use the data →
Official statistics or surveyFigures published by an official statistics body, usually based on a survey or statistical analysis.
CensusA population count based on the questions and definitions used in the census.
Public-service recordsFigures taken from benefits, services or other public records.
ResearchA finding from a study or organisation. We name the source and do not present it as an official statistic.
