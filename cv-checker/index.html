Support that respects you

You don’t need to tell us about a disability. Your CV can include paid work, volunteering, caring experience and community projects where relevant.

Feedback uses text labels, not colour alone. All controls work with a keyboard. BSL video guidance is not yet available.

Your CV stays with you

This page processes your CV and job advert locally and does not upload either to a server or save them to an account. Clear everything removes your text from this page. Job-fit scoring also runs in your browser.

File-reading tools and the optional AI model load from external providers, who receive normal connection information such as your IP address, but not your CV text. The AI model may be cached by your browser; clear site data to remove it.

Downloads remain on your device. AI suggestions need your review: never add skills or achievements that aren’t yours.
